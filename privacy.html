DeliCad Express Tools for Mac does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from its users. The plugin operates entirely offline within AutoCAD for Mac.

1. Who We Are

DeliCad Express Tools for Mac is developed and maintained by Andreas Delitzakis ("we", "us", "our"). This plugin is distributed via the Autodesk App Store.

Contact: andreasdelitzakis@gmail.com

2. Data We Do Not Collect

This plugin does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, address)
  • Drawing files, project data, or design content
  • Usage statistics, telemetry, or analytics
  • Device information or hardware identifiers
  • Location data
  • Any data transmitted to external servers

3. Local Trial File

The free trial version of DeliCad Express Tools stores a single file locally on your computer to track the trial period start date. This file:

  • Is stored inside the plugin's own bundle folder on your Mac
  • Contains only a single number (the Julian date of first launch)
  • Is never transmitted or shared with anyone
  • Is automatically deleted when you uninstall the plugin

4. Third-Party Services

DeliCad Express Tools for Mac does not integrate with or communicate with any third-party services, APIs, or external servers. All functionality operates locally within AutoCAD for Mac.

Please refer to Autodesk's Privacy Statement for information on how the Autodesk App Store itself handles your data: autodesk.com/privacy

5. Children's Privacy

DeliCad Express Tools is intended for professional and semi-professional users of AutoCAD for Mac. It is not directed at individuals under the age of 18 and we do not knowingly collect any data from minors.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the plugin after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal information to access, correct, or delete. If you have any privacy-related questions, please contact us directly.
